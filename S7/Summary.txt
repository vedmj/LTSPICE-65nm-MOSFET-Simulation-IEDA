Based on the Voltage Transfer Characteristic (VTC) plot obtained from sweeping Vin from 0 V to VDD
​
1. Logical Inversion (Complementary States)
When Vin = 0 V (Logic LOW): The pMOS transistor is turned ON while the nMOS transistor is turned OFF. 
This pulls the output node up to VDD=1.1 V (Logic HIGH).   
When Vin = 1.1 V (Logic HIGH): The pMOS transistor turns OFF while the nMOS transistor turns ON. This pulls the output node down to 
0 V (Logic LOW).   
Hence it performs at NOT gate

2. Sharp Transition Region
The steep negative slope in the center of the curve shows that as Vin passes through the threshold region (around 0.55 V), the 
circuit sharply switches state. This high voltage gain inside the transition region ensures clear separation between logical 0 and 
logical 1 levels, providing high noise margins for digital applications.
